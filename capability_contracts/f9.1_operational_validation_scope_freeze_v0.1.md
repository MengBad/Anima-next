## 1. Context

F8.1–F8.13 closed the capability-governance build-out and froze its boundaries
(F8.13 §5–§9). Foundation Evolution Protocol F2 permits amendment only on an
**observed contradiction** — never on a better idea. F9.1 was authorized to look
for one, read-only, over the existing chain.

---

## 2. Decision

**F9.1 concludes INCONCLUSIVE BY CONSTRUCTION.**

Not "the design passed". Not "the design failed". The instrument required to
answer the question does not exist in the running system.

Three findings, in order of weight:

1. **The observation window is empty.** Zero samples across Resolution, Health
   and Recovery, over a continuously-active service (uptime 1d 19h 23m at survey
   time), against 243,192 journal lines containing zero governance events.

2. **Emptiness is the ruled state, not a deviation.** F8.7 Q2 form (a) makes the
   audit sink an external injection with zero production diff; F8.10.1 deferred
   evidence wiring to a separate authorization; F8.12 ships `POLICIES` empty as
   an explicit ruling. Nobody has ever been authorized to switch observation on.

3. **The production runtime predates the entire F-series.** `/opt/anima` newest
   `.py` is 2026-07-30; `anima.capability`, `anima.runtime` and `anima.contracts`
   are not importable there. Even a fully wired repo would emit nothing in
   production today.

Consequently: **zero qualified contradictions**, and the Foundation stays frozen.

## 5. Scope decision (the open question, framed but NOT decided)

F9.1 cannot be repeated with a different outcome. Re-running the same read-only
survey next week returns the same zeros, because nothing in the system will have
changed by itself — there is no daemon, by design.

Therefore exactly one branch point exists, and it belongs to the owner:

| Option | Consequence | Authorization state |
|-|-|-|
| **A. Stay dormant** | Governance remains a documented, tested, unexercised design. F2 stays unsatisfiable. Zero risk, zero learning. | Default. Requires no action. |
| **B. Authorize a minimal observation-enabling slice (F9.2)** | Deploy current HEAD + inject one sink in a controlled window. Would produce the first real samples. Requires closing G-01 first, and is a *code change* — outside every boundary of F9.1. | **NOT AUTHORIZED.** Would need its own scope freeze. |

This ADR **frames** the choice and **does not make it**. Recommending B here
would be exactly the "better idea" that F2 forbids as a change driver.

If B is ever authorized, the observed prerequisites are already known:
G-01 (config field), G-02 (deploy lag), and a decision on which single chain to
observe first. Those are inputs to a future proposal, not commitments made here.

## 7. Closing position

The most valuable output of F9.1 is a negative result, stated plainly:
**the governance chain is unfalsifiable in its current deployment posture.**

It is coherent, tested at 177 passing tests, and internally consistent — and it
has never met a real request. Recording that honestly is worth more than a
manufactured contradiction, because the protocol that consumes contradictions is
only as trustworthy as the restraint used when none is found.

**STOP.**
