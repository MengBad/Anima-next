## Why no RFC-0112

The Foundation investigation (RFC-0100 §1 noun table + RFC-0100B §2 Authority Matrix) shows:

- The nine Life-layer nouns — Identity, Need, Affect, Drive, Goal, Reflection, **Learning**,
  Relationship, Memory — are all consumed by RFC-0101 through RFC-0111.
- Persona (RFC-0110) is a *derived projection* of Identity, not a base noun; it has no row in the
  Authority Matrix.
- The only Life noun without a dedicated Life RFC is **Learning (§1.11)**, but it is already:
  - **named** in the Ontology (§1.11);
  - **without an independent Authority** — its durable change is realized as
    `Reflection → Memory[Skill / Preference / Semantic]`;
  - creating a "Learning State" or "Learning Authority" would violate **O1** (single authority).
- The remaining Ontology nouns (Task, Decision Engine, Planner, Workflow, Execution, Capability,
  Provider) belong to the **Cognition / Infrastructure** layers and are out of scope for the Life-layer
  RFC track (they live in the LIMBot V2 RFC-000x track).

Therefore **no RFC-0112 candidate exists**. The track closes by *exhaustion*, not by cancellation.
